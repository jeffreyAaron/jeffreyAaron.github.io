Overview

This project is about building intuition for how perspective, focal length, zoom, and center of projection affect a photo — through three hands-on shooting exercises.

Part 1: Selfie comparison

A close-up selfie versus a portrait taken from farther away with zoom, keeping the face roughly the same size in frame.

Explain why the zoomed-in, farther-away shot looks better — what’s happening to facial proportions as the camera gets closer.

Part 2: Architectural perspective

The same building photographed twice: once zoomed in from far away, once up close without zoom, keeping the building roughly the same size in frame both times.

Explain the compression/flattening effect — how the perceived depth and converging lines of the building change between the two shots.

Part 3: Dolly zoom

A sequence of stills taken while walking backward and zooming in simultaneously, keeping the subject roughly the same size throughout, combined into an animated GIF.

Describe what’s happening to the background relative to the subject as you dolly out and zoom in.
