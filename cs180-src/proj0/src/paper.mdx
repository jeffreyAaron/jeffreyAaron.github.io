## Part 1: Selfie comparison

<TwoColumns>
  <Picture slot="left" src={selfieClose} alt="Close-up selfie-style portrait" />
  <Picture slot="right" src={selfieFar} alt="Portrait taken from farther away, zoomed in" />
</TwoColumns>

In the close up selfie, the edges of my face are distorted and stretched by the camera. 
The nose and mouse is much closer to the camera and that appears much larger. This makes the faces look oval in space.
By going farther away, the face is smoothed out by the noise from the increased distance and the face appears more flat.

## Part 2: Architectural perspective

<TwoColumns>
  <Picture slot="left" src={buildingZoomed} alt="Building photographed up close without zoom" />
  <Picture slot="right" src={buildingClose} alt="Building photographed with zoom from far away" />
</TwoColumns>

Similar to the image of the face, the close up photo without zoom (left) shows a lot of depth. The face of building has a larger skew from large to small as you go farther. 
On the right however, with the zoom, it looks flat. This is because the camera is farther away and any changes in depth of the face of the building or the wall are less noticable to the camera.
